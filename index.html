OPEN-SOURCE PROTOTYPE · PEPG 2.0

A common board for public problems.

PEPG is a governance model. The PEPG Board is an open-source place where public problems receive a persistent identity, can be verified and revised, and can connect to people, institutions, solutions, implementation and oversight.

PEPG principle
Problem→People→Action

THE MODEL

How PEPG Board works

01

Register

A person submits a public problem and receives a persistent Problem ID.

02

Verify

The community or a trusted authority can provide evidence and verification.

03

Improve

People can propose a sub-problem, improved definition, separate problem, or challenge.

04

Connect

Solutions, funding, implementation and oversight can happen on other platforms and systems.
