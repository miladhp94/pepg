## What is PEPG?

**Platform-Enabled People Governance (PEPG)** is a conceptual model for organizing public problem-solving around the **problem itself**, rather than around a single government institution, bureaucracy, or digital platform.

PEPG connects citizens, experts, government institutions, implementers, funders, contractors, and oversight participants around specific public problems.

The goal is simple:

> **Put the problem at the center and connect the people and resources needed to solve it.**
